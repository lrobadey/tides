03 / Lifecycle

One grain, from birth to release.

Schedule the birth

Derive the average launch interval from Density and Size, then add deterministic jitter so births remain even enough for coverage without revealing a mechanical clock.

Choose the source

Start near the moving centroid inside the Time history. Tide selects how far behind it the birth may trail; Drift adds a small bounded deviation.

Capture the state

Store source position, playback speed, pan, lifetime, and envelope shape at birth. Automation after this point belongs to later generations.

Read and shape once

Advance through the buffer for the grain’s Size-derived lifetime while its envelope moves from attack through body to release.

Spill into the cohort

Keep sounding while newer grains arrive. Different ages and retained birth states create the overlapping wake without one shared amplitude crest.

Finish permanently

Release cleanly, become inactive, and return the voice slot to the pool. A later event is a new grain with new birth state, never a restarted cycle.
